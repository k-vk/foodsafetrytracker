How records are collected and published

Source hierarchy

  1. Official FSSAI records
  2. State food-safety departments
  3. Municipal / health authorities
  4. Official inspection, seizure or laboratory records
  5. Reputable news reports
  6. Official government social accounts, as pointers to primary action

Publication rule

  • Records are updated daily at 08:00 IST.
  • Official-source records are published directly.
  • News-based records are published only when they show an explicit regulatory action and a specific finding; they are marked "corroboration recommended".
  • Uncertain items are held in a review queue and are not shown here.

Evidence principles

  • Sample collection is not treated as a finding of guilt.
  • Every record keeps its source link and an evidence excerpt.
  • Compliance and corrective outcomes stay visible alongside earlier findings.
  • No safety scores, "worst" lists, rankings or permanent labels.
Extraction is rule-based and may contain errors. Always check the cited source before relying on a record. Establishments can request a correction or add a follow-up outcome by contacting the maintainers of this repository.
