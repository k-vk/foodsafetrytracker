How to read this database

A listing records what an identified source reported or what an official record documents. It is not a permanent judgement about a business. Primary government records are preferred. News-only records are labelled as such. Follow-up inspections, corrections and licence restoration are retained so the history is not one-sided.

The tracker does not publish anonymous allegations, user reviews or unverified social-media claims as findings.
